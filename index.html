Rich Heritage & Economy

Nashik: Spiritual, Agricultural &
Industrial Powerhouse

Located in northern Maharashtra on the banks of the sacred River Godavari, Nashik is one of the four holy cities hosting the grand Simhastha Kumbh Mela. Beyond its deep spiritual roots in Ramayana history, Nashik is a leading industrial engine, defense manufacturing hub (HAL Ozar), and India's agricultural giant for grapes and onions.

Spiritual & Ramayana Heritage

Home to Panchavati, Ramkund, Kalaram Temple, Sita Gufa, and Trimbakeshwar (one of the 12 Jyotirlingas). Millions visit during the sacred Simhastha Kumbh Mela every 12 years

Kalaram & Trimbakeshwar Belt
Wine Capital of India

Nashik produces over 80% of India's wine, hosting over 50 wineries including Sula, Grover Zampa, and York. Its fertile soil yields table-grade grapes exported worldwide

Sula Vineyards & Export Hub
Lasalgaon Onion APMC

Lasalgaon in Nashik district is Asia's largest wholesale market for onions, dictating onion trade across the Indian subcontinent along with sugarcane and pomegranates.

Asia's Largest APMC Yard
Industrial & Defense Hub

Satpur, Ambad, and Sinnar MIDC belts power manufacturing, automobile, and defense production including Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL) fighter aircraft facility at Ozar.
