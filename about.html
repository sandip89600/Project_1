Welcome to Vani Village

Nestled at the foothills of the Sahyadri mountain range in Nashik district, Vani is a major historical and commercial village famous as a base for pilgrimage to the holy Saptashrungi Nivasini Devi Temple (Gad). Vani is also an agricultural hub, celebrated for its grape vineyards and onion markets.
