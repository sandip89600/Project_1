CITIZEN GRIEVANCE CELL

Public Complaint & Suggestion Box

Your village matters. Directly report issues related to drinking water, broken street lights, road potholes, sanitation, or scheme delays to the Gram Panchayat and Block Development Officer.

  1. Submit Details: Fill in the village name, category, and issue description.
  2. Ticket Issued: Receive an instant tracking ID for status follow-up.
  3. Resolution: Gram Sevak or Junior Engineer inspects within 72 hours.
All submissions are monitored under the Public Service Guarantee Act.
